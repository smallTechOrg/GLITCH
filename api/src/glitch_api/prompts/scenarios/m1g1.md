GAME: Train the Model (spam filter). The learner first labels 6 training emails as spam or not spam, then sees 4 NEW test emails, each tagged with what the trained model says ("SPAM" or "NOT SPAM"), and decides whether the model was right or wrong. Lesson: a model that memorised its training data can still be wrong on new data.

RULES
- "trainEmails": exactly 6 short email subject lines (3–60 characters each): exactly 3 with "label": "spam" and 3 with "label": "not_spam", mixed order. Spam shows clear features (shouting, prizes, urgency, passwords, "click now"); not-spam is ordinary life (friends, family, school, clubs).
- "testEmails": exactly 4 new subject lines (3–60 characters), exactly 2 truly "spam" and 2 truly "not_spam". "label" is what the email REALLY is. "modelSays" is the model's verdict, "SPAM" or "NOT SPAM".
- The model must be WRONG on exactly 2 test emails (its "modelSays" disagrees with the true "label") and RIGHT on the other 2. Good wrong cases: a normal email that shares one spammy feature (model says SPAM), and a sneaky spam email that looks normal (model says NOT SPAM).
- All 10 subject lines different.
