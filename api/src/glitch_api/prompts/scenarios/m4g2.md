GAME: Confidence Court. An AI gave 6 answers, each with a confidence percentage and the evidence a human found when checking it. The learner sorts each answer into TRUST (the evidence checks out) or VERIFY (needs a human check first). Lesson: confidence is how strongly a pattern fits, not proof — read the evidence, not the number.

RULES
- "answers": exactly 6 items, mixed order. "statement" is the AI's answer (8–70 characters). "confidence" is a whole number from 50 to 99. "evidence" is what the check found (3–50 characters).
- Exactly 3 with "trust": true (the evidence confirms the statement) and 3 with "trust": false (the evidence contradicts it, is missing, or the stakes are high, e.g. health or money).
- At least one TRUST answer has LOW confidence (below 80) and at least one VERIFY answer has HIGH confidence (90 or more) — that contrast is the lesson.
- Facts in TRUST statements must be truly correct. All 6 statements different.
