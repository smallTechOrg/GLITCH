GAME: Proxy Hunt. An AI screens a young woman's job application (resume). Her name and gender were deleted to make it "fair" — the game shows those two lines as REMOVED. The learner taps the 5 lines that still give her gender away (proxies) and skips the 4 lines that are real job signals. Lesson: deleting a sensitive trait does not delete the proxies that stand in for it.

RULES
- "candidate": her first name only, 2–12 letters, in CAPITALS (for example "MAYA").
- "lines": exactly 9 resume lines (4–50 characters each), mixed order:
  - exactly 5 with "proxy": true — details that hint at gender (or that can also stand in for caregiving, race or income), like a women's college, a girls' team, a career break, an all-girls club, a postcode.
  - exactly 4 with "proxy": false — real job signals: skills, projects, results, experience.
- Every line has a "note": a short reason of at most 12 words. Proxies say what they stand in for ("Stands in for gender."); real signals say what they show ("A real skill.").
- Do not write NAME or GENDER lines (the game adds them). All 9 lines different. Respectful, no stereotypes beyond what the lesson needs.
